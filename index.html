Embedded Systems / IoT / Digital Inputs

Atelier 7: Classic Digital Input - External Pull-down Resistor

Arduino / C++ Digital Input (INPUT) External Pull-down Resistor (10kΩ) Direct Logic (HIGH/LOW)

Technical and practical study of digital input wiring on Arduino using an external 10 kΩ pull-down resistor. Unlike internal configuration, this physical setup explicitly anchors the signal pin to low level (GND / 0V) at rest, establishing direct reading logic (*Straight Logic*: Button pressed = HIGH / 1).

Hardware Architecture & Direct Digital Logic

  • Pull-down Resistor Principle: Inserting a 10 kΩ resistor (brown-black-orange) between the signal pin (Pin 2) and ground (GND), acting as a drain to clear residual charges and maintain 0V until the button is activated.
  • Detailed Physical Wiring: Powering breadboard rails (5V and GND), placing the push-button across the central groove, supplying 5V to one terminal, connecting signal wire to Arduino digital pin 2, and anchoring the pull-down resistor to the negative rail.
  • Straight Logic in C++: Configuring the pin in standard mode (pinMode(pinBouton, INPUT)) and applying direct conditional processing via digitalRead(), where HIGH (1) distinctly corresponds to button actuation to activate the LED output.
